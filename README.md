# Hotel Performance Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze hotel transaction data and provide business-focused insights into revenue, bookings, pricing, customer segments, booking channels, regional performance, sales trends, and customer satisfaction.

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates a complete Business Intelligence workflow using Microsoft Power BI, from data preparation and exploratory analysis to dashboard development and business insight generation.

The hotel transaction dataset was prepared and analyzed using Power Query before being transformed into an interactive dashboard containing ten key visualizations.

The analysis covers three hotels and examines multiple aspects of hotel business performance, including financial performance, booking activity, customer behavior, regional performance, pricing, and service quality.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall hotel revenue and booking performance
- Compare sales performance across hotels
- Examine regional revenue distribution
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Evaluate average daily rate (ADR)
- Understand revenue contribution by customer segment
- Analyze booking channel performance
- Compare customer satisfaction across hotels
- Present business insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard

## Dashboard

The final dashboard contains 10 key visualizations:

1. **Total Sales** — KPI showing approximately RM 46.78M in total sales
2. **Total Bookings** — KPI showing approximately 6,000 bookings
3. **Average Daily Rate (ADR)** — Average ADR of approximately RM 84.59
4. **Average Customer Rating** — Average rating of approximately 3.69
5. **Sales by Hotel** — Comparison of revenue across hotels
6. **Sales by Region** — Regional revenue comparison
7. **Monthly Sales Trend** — Month-to-month revenue trend
8. **Sales by Customer Segment** — Revenue contribution from customer segments
9. **Sales by Booking Channel** — Revenue generated through different booking channels
10. **Average Customer Rating by Hotel** — Customer satisfaction comparison across hotels

These visualizations were selected to communicate the main business patterns identified during the analysis. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

## Data Preparation

Power Query was used during the data preparation stage to:

- Review and clean column names
- Check and assign appropriate data types
- Remove unnecessary leading and trailing spaces
- Perform data quality checks
- Prepare the dataset for analysis and visualization

The exploratory analysis was then used to identify meaningful relationships and determine which visualizations best communicated the business story. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

## Key Business Insights

The dashboard provides a high-level view of hotel performance through:

- Overall revenue and booking volume
- Pricing performance through ADR
- Revenue differences between hotels and regions
- Monthly changes in sales
- Revenue contribution from customer segments
- Performance of booking channels
- Differences in customer satisfaction between hotels

The analysis is designed to move from overall business performance toward more detailed customer and service-related insights. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
